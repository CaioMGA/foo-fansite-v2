### What is foo-sdb
The **First of October Song Database** (**foo-sdb**) is a spreadsheet that catalogs every song recorded by **[Andrew Huang](https://www.youtube.com/@andrewhuang)** and **[Rob Scallon](https://www.youtube.com/@robscallon/)** for **First of October**.

For each song it keeps the YouTube link, title, album year, length, genres, themes, instruments, lead and backing vocals, sound engineer and recording location. It also holds the playlists that feed the [Radio](/radio).
